# What is Gizu?

Gizu is an **any chain confidential investment app** for iOS and Android. It lets you invest in tokenized stocks and yield vaults onchain, without putting your portfolio on public display.

Onchain investing is normally fully public: anyone who knows your wallet address can see what you hold, what you buy and when. Gizu keeps your investments off the record by moving them through a confidential balance and spreading them across fresh wallets that only you control.

## What makes Gizu different

- **A passkey wallet, no seed phrase.** You create and unlock your wallet with Face ID, fingerprint or a supported passkey manager. Your keys stay on your device.
- **Confidential by design.** Your investments start from a confidential balance that block explorers can't read, so they are much harder to trace back to you.
- **Any chain, one app.** Gizu handles the chains, bridges and wallets in between. You choose what to invest in.
- **You stay in control.** Nothing moves without your approval on your own device.
