# Adding funds

Gizu investments start from **USDC**. Add USDC to your wallet once, and you can invest it across every supported chain from the same balance.

## Send USDC from an exchange or another wallet

1. In Gizu, open your wallet and copy your receiving address.
2. In your exchange or other wallet, send USDC **on Monad** to that address.
3. The balance appears in Gizu once the transfer confirms.

:::warning Use the right network
Send USDC on the network shown in the app. Funds sent on a different network may not arrive in your Gizu balance.
:::

## Buy USDC with a card or bank transfer

Card and bank top-ups are coming soon. Until then, buy USDC on an exchange and send it to your Gizu wallet.

## What happens to your funds

The USDC you add sits in a regular wallet balance and stays there until you decide what to invest in. Nothing is moved, shielded or invested until you confirm an investment in the app. See [How Gizu works](./how-it-works.md#why-the-order-matters).
