# FAQ

### How does Gizu make my investments private?

Your investments start from a confidential balance that block explorers can't read, and each one lands in fresh wallets controlled by your passkey. Together, these make it much harder to connect the money you add with what you hold. See [How privacy works](./privacy.md).

### What can other people see?

Money arriving in your wallet and transfers to your investment wallets are public transactions. Your confidential balance is not visible as an ordinary balance. Providers can observe parts of an operation, and timing or amounts can still reveal relationships. Gizu does not promise complete anonymity.

### Who controls my money?

You do. Gizu is a self-custody wallet: your keys are derived on your device from your passkey, and every investment, sale and withdrawal needs your approval.

### Do I need a seed phrase?

No. Your passkey replaces the seed phrase. To recover your wallet on a new phone, you need your encrypted backup and your original passkey. See [Passkey wallet](./passkey-wallet.md#backup-and-recovery).

### Which chains does Gizu support?

Currently Ethereum, Monad and Robinhood Chain. See [Supported chains & protocols](./supported-networks.mdx).

### Where do vault returns come from?

From the underlying strategy of the vault you choose. Review its curator, assets and risks before investing. Returns vary and are not guaranteed.

### Can I lose money?

Yes. Privacy protects information about your investments; it does not remove investment risk.
