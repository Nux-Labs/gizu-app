# How Gizu works

From creating your wallet to following your position, a Gizu investment takes six steps. You make the decisions; Gizu handles the chains and keeps your investments off the record.

<ol className="gizu-steps">
  <li>
    <strong>A passkey wallet. No seed phrase.</strong><br />
    Create and unlock your wallet with Face ID, fingerprint or a supported passkey manager. Your keys stay on your device, and nothing moves without your approval. <a href="./passkey-wallet">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Add funds your way.</strong><br />
    Add USDC to your wallet. Top up once, invest across any chain. <a href="./adding-funds">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Choose where your capital goes.</strong><br />
    Tokenized stocks, yield vaults and more, in one place. Review the asset, fees and limits, then confirm the amount. <a href="./investing">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Shielded before it's invested.</strong><br />
    Once you confirm, your USDC moves into a confidential balance that block explorers can't read. Every investment starts from there. <a href="./privacy">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Spread across fresh wallets.</strong><br />
    Each investment lands in one to five new wallets, all controlled by your passkey. No single address shows the full picture. <a href="./privacy#fresh-wallets">Learn more</a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Follow your position.</strong><br />
    Every holding, across every wallet and chain, in one live portfolio. <a href="./portfolio">Learn more</a>
  </li>
</ol>

## Why the order matters

The USDC you add to your wallet sits in a regular, public balance. It is only shielded when you confirm an investment. That is why choosing what to invest in comes before the confidential step: Gizu prepares the route for that specific investment, shows it to you for review and only then asks for your approval.
