# Portfolio & selling

## Follow your position

Your investments are spread across several wallets and chains, but you don't have to keep track of them. Gizu shows every holding in one portfolio, valued in USD, together with the progress of any operation that is still running.

If Gizu can't read a balance at a given moment, for example because a network is slow to respond, the portfolio marks that value as unavailable instead of guessing.

## Selling

When you sell a holding, Gizu sells it from the wallets that hold it and returns the proceeds to your **confidential balance**. The money stays shielded and is ready for your next investment.

## Withdrawing

Moving money out of your confidential balance into a regular, public wallet is a separate action. When you withdraw, Gizu sends the amount you choose to fresh receiving wallets, and you approve it with your passkey like any other operation.
